Valkiri #319

Previously Valkyvoir. Green is the normal palette and blue is the shiny palette, reusing approved PRL sprites without recolouring. Gardevoir evolves with an Ancient Stone at level 45.

Build verified; in-game palette/evolution QA outstanding

Sound moves

Gardevoir gains Round at level 36 and Hyper Voice at 38. Valkiri can relearn both and gains Boomburst on evolution and at level 55.

Build verified; in-game learn prompts QA outstanding

Throat Spray

Build verified; shop/effect QA outstanding

Noxichu #032

Pikachu's Moon Stone alternative evolution learns Bite immediately. Approved newer PRL battle artwork and party icon replace the older PJR sprites.

Build verified; newest sprite QA outstanding

Evolutions

Johto rebalance

202 trainer parties adjusted. Wild encounters cover 37 Johto areas and 63 day/night variants, including 145 slots using appropriate evolved species.

Build verified; encounter QA outstanding

Steevee & Neevee

These are recurring human trainers, not species: Route 37 (replaces Ann & Anne, levels 30–32), Route 47 (38–40), Route 44 (44–47), and Kanto Route 3 (61–64). All four use the approved PRL custom Eevee Twins battle intro.

Build verified; actual encounter/intro QA outstanding

Johto Gym Leaders

Eight upgraded first-encounter teams with signature Relics. £10,000, a TM and an item per leader; full team roster in the linked Google Sheet.
