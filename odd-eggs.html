ROUTE 34 · SECRET NURSERY RHYMES

Odd Egg passwords

At the Day-Care south of Goldenrod City, the clerk asks for a four-word Easy Chat answer. A separate NPC outdoors, surrounded by four wandering baby Pokémon, shares mysterious nursery rhymes without explaining what they unlock.

Upcoming feature, not yet in downloadable Alpha 3. The four custom eggs and their clues are in the PRU development source. A ROM build and in-game tests are still required.

How to claim an egg

Speak to the Day-Care couple's son, ensure you have space in your party, enter the four words in order, then hatch the egg. The outdoor nursery-rhyme NPC can be spoken to repeatedly.

Purchased Mystery Eggs: nine classic starters

The separate Day-Care seller charges ¥5,000 for a uniformly random starter egg: Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle, Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile, Treecko, Torchic or Mudkip. The four PRU baby Pokémon are no longer in purchased eggs; they are obtained through the four secret-word eggs instead.

This is a development-source change, not yet included in public Alpha 3.
