ROUTE 34 · DAY-CARE EGG GUIDE

Odd Egg passwords

At the Day-Care south of Goldenrod City, speak to the Day-Care couple's son to claim a special egg. The current PRU egg system uses the GBA typing keyboard: enter one word for the Pokémon you want. The codes are not case-sensitive.

Build compatibility: This guide covers the newer Alpha 4.2 / Alpha 5 test-era egg system, not the old four-part Easy Chat system or the public Alpha 3 release. Kan's egg has been obtained in testing; the Baby Expert's JOEY clue dialogue still needs its final in-game check.

How to claim an egg

  1. Go to the Route 34 Day-Care, south of Goldenrod City, and speak to the Day-Care couple's son.
  2. Make room in your party for the Egg.
  3. When the GBA keyboard appears, type one password from the tables above and confirm it.
  4. Hatch the Egg normally. Each choice is subject to its own claim limit.

Need a hint instead? Speak to the Baby Expert outdoors, near the baby Pokémon overworld sprites. The clues are deliberately mysterious; the Baby Kan clue is meant to give JOEY.

Purchased Mystery Eggs: the nine classic starters

A separate Day-Care seller offers Mystery Eggs for ¥5,000 each. These contain only one of the nine regular Kanto, Johto or Hoenn starters, chosen at random:

Kanto: Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle

Johto: Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile

Hoenn: Treecko, Torchic, Mudkip

The custom baby Pokémon cannot hatch from purchased Mystery Eggs. Each of the nine starters has an equal chance in the updated source; check your ROM version before relying on this distribution.
