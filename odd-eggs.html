ROUTE 34 · DAY-CARE EGG GUIDE

Odd Egg passwords

At the Day-Care south of Goldenrod City, speak to the Day-Care couple's son to claim a special egg. The current PRU egg system uses the GBA typing keyboard: enter one word for the Pokémon you want. The codes are not case-sensitive.

Alpha 5: The nine Kanto, Johto and Hoenn starter lines are egg-only. Your existing save can claim each starter's Odd Egg once with its own password; the separate ¥5,000 Mystery Egg remains available.

These custom baby Pokémon are obtained from the secret egg system, rather than wild encounters or purchased Mystery Eggs. Each has its own once-per-save claim, so you can collect all five.

How to claim an egg

  1. Go to the Route 34 Day-Care, south of Goldenrod City, and speak to the Day-Care couple's son.
  2. Make room in your party for the Egg.
  3. When the GBA keyboard appears, type one password from the tables on this page and confirm it.
  4. Hatch the Egg normally. The classic seven share one claim; the custom babies and nine starters each have independent claims.

For custom baby clues, speak to the Baby Expert outdoors, near the baby Pokémon overworld sprites. Trainers with Cyndaquil in Azalea Town and Totodile in Goldenrod City also tell you their Egg passwords.

Nine egg-only starters

For a specific starter, enter its name as a single password with the Day-Care couple's son. Each can be claimed once per save. None of these starter lines appears as a wild encounter in Alpha 5.

Kanto: Bulbasaur, Charmander, Squirtle

Johto: Chikorita, Cyndaquil, Totodile

Hoenn: Treecko, Torchic, Mudkip
