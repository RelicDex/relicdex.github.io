About the project

The next chapter of RelicDex.

A free, community-created companion for Pokémon Relic Universe. We are building the destination now and will fill it with approved, tested information once the game and Pokédex are complete.

Work in progress
Launch sequence
01 · PREPARED

RelicDex site foundation

Mobile-friendly layout, accessible navigation, working search/filter interfaces and future-proof data model. The initial pages deliberately contain no provisional Pokémon information.

02 · WAITING

Import the finished PRU Pokédex

Populate the definitive species roster, evolution chains, typings, stats, moves, abilities and approved original sprite files, including trainer-exclusive artwork inside the original species entry.

03 · VERIFY

Check locations and item availability

Confirm every catch route, encounter condition, shop, held item, evolution method and badge-gated unlock against PRU gameplay.

04 · RELEASE

Activate the full Team Builder

Enable story-aware recommendations, competitive-style completed sets, saveable six-Pokémon parties and offline PWA functionality.

Good to know

Can I install RelicDex on iPhone or Android?

Yes, once published over HTTPS, the website is designed to be installable as a Progressive Web App through supported browsers. A separate Android APK remains an optional later project.

Will it cost anything?

The planned static website is free to host on GitHub Pages. We intend to use a memorable short link, but the tinyurl.com/RelicDex alias is only proposed and has not been reserved or activated.

Where is the Pokédex information coming from?

From the final, user-approved PRU Pokédex and subsequent gameplay checks. The current site is intentionally empty of species data to avoid publishing incorrect catch locations, learnsets or items.

Will my team data be private?

The Team Builder is designed to store saved selections locally in your browser. There is no login, account or tracking requirement in this preview.

Is this an official Pokémon website?

No. It is an unofficial, non-commercial fan resource, not endorsed by or affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Game Freak or Creatures Inc.
